隐私说明

更新于 2026 年 10 月 16 日

一句话：拍猫不需要你的任何个人信息。

我们不收集什么

  • 不用注册、不用登录。
  • 不放广告，不放第三方统计或追踪代码，不用 Cookie。
  • 你拍了多少下、分享图长什么样，都只在你的手机里算，不上传。

存在你手机上的

  • 你选的语言（中文/English）存在浏览器本地存储里，只为下次打开时记住。清除浏览器数据即可删掉。

网站托管

网站放在 Cloudflare 上。和所有网站一样，Cloudflare 会为安全和运行处理访问请求的基础信息（如 IP 地址、浏览器类型），我们不额外读取或保存这些信息。

打赏

如果你选择打赏，付款在 Waffo 的收银台完成（Waffo 是代收款的商户）。卡号等支付信息由 Waffo 处理，我们看不到；我们只会收到订单所需的信息（例如邮箱、金额），只用于对账和处理退款。

联系

有疑问或想删除与你有关的订单信息，请在 GitHub 提 Issue。

Privacy

Updated October 16, 2026

In short: patting the cat needs none of your personal data.

What we don't collect

  • No sign-up, no login.
  • No ads, no third-party analytics or trackers, no cookies.
  • Your pat count and share card are computed on your phone and never uploaded.

What stays on your device

  • Your language choice is kept in the browser's local storage so the site remembers it. Clearing site data removes it.

Hosting

The site runs on Cloudflare. Like any website host, Cloudflare processes basic request data (such as IP address and browser type) for security and operation. We don't read or keep it beyond that.

Tips

If you choose to tip, payment happens on Waffo's checkout (Waffo is the merchant of record). Waffo handles your card details — we never see them. We only receive what an order needs (such as email and amount), used for bookkeeping and refunds.

Contact

Questions, or want order data about you deleted? Open an issue on GitHub.
