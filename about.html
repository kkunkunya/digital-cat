常见问题FAQ

手机没声音？No sound on my phone?

先点一下屏幕——浏览器要你碰过页面才肯出声。再看看 iPhone 侧边的静音拨片和媒体音量，以及左上角的声音按钮是不是关着。

Tap the screen once — browsers only play sound after you touch the page. Then check the iPhone silent switch, your media volume, and the sound button at the top left.

要下载或登录吗？Do I need an app or an account?

不用。打开网页就能拍，手机电脑都行。

No. Open the page and start patting — on a phone or a computer.

要花钱吗？Does it cost anything?

免费，没有广告。玩完一轮，结果卡里可以自愿请猫猫吃个罐罐：一次性 US$2.99，不订阅、不自动扣费，见条款。

It's free, with no ads. After a round, the result card lets you treat the cat if you like: a one-time US$2.99, no subscription, no auto-renewal. See the terms.

会收集我的信息吗？Do you collect my data?

不收集个人信息，没有追踪代码，详见隐私说明。

We don't collect personal data and run no trackers. See the privacy note.
