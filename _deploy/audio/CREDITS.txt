# Audio credits

Every sound in the current build is a real recording. Nothing is synthesized.

## Used by the site

Kun's own cat (`audio/kun/`, recorded by Kun on an iPhone, used with Kun's permission):
- `p1`–`p5` — real pats on the cat's back/butt, from `IMG_4953.MOV` (the clip the promo video opens with). 0.16 s each, room hum high-passed, light denoise, levelled. One plays on every pat.
- `m1`–`m4` — the cat's own short meows, from `IMG_4943.MOV` (at 0.0 s, 7.6 s, 13.0 s, 20.1 s). Speech-free spots only, picked with the AudioSet sound classifier (MIT/ast-finetuned-audioset) and checked on the spectrogram; high-passed, light denoise, faded, levelled. One plays every 5–6 pats, never closer than 2.5 s however fast you pat. Coal plays the same meows 10 % lower.

Kun's videos contain no audible purr (the classifier finds 0 purr in all three), so the purr comes from public recordings:
- `purr_orange.mp3` (Tangerine) — from "Whiskers' purr edit.ogg", Wikimedia Commons, Adam Cuerden (edit by X!), Public Domain. Trimmed, levelled, EQ'd.
- `purr_black.mp3` (Coal) — from "Purring cat.oga", Wikimedia Commons, Mysid, Public Domain. Trimmed, EQ'd.

The purr is never played as a loop: `src/main.js` re-sequences the recording's own breath cycles (`cuts`) at random, swells it slowly while you pat and fades it slowly after you stop. Phone speakers can't play the ~25 Hz rumble, so a copy of the low band is gently saturated to make it audible (processing of the recording, not a synthesized sound).
